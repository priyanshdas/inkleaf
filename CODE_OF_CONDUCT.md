# Code of conduct

Inkleaf is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of
experience. Everyone should be able to take part without being put down.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that a "no" to a feature isn't a "no" to you.
- Write so that someone reading in their second language can follow.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Discriminating against anyone for who they are: their age, body, disability,
  ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, religion, caste,
  sexual orientation, or anything else about them.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Where it applies

In the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent
the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, Priyansh Das, privately through the email on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/priyanshdas). Reports are kept
confidential. Depending on what happened, the response can be anything from a
quiet word to a permanent ban from the project.
