Everything InkLeaf PDF does

Every tool is free and works offline. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose a feature that has to go online, like sending a file.

Change the text

  • Edit text: click any line and retype it, in a matching font. The old words are removed from the file when you save.
  • Add text anywhere, with your choice of font, size, colour, bold and italic.
  • Redact words, areas or every match of a search. Redacted text and pictures are deleted from the file, not covered.
  • Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and more are typed and saved properly.
  • Find words across the whole document, scanned pages included once they’re recognised.

Fill and sign

  • Fill in PDF forms: text boxes, checkboxes, choices and dates.
  • Make forms of your own, with fields people can fill in.
  • Draw, type or upload a signature and initials. Photos of a paper signature lose their background.
  • Saved signatures and initials are ready next time.

Mark it up

  • Pen, highlighter, shapes, lines and arrows.
  • Comments and stamps.
  • Pictures, page numbers, headers, footers and watermarks.
  • Everything you add can be moved, resized, restyled or deleted later.

Pages

  • Merge PDFs, insert pages from other files, split, extract, delete, reorder and rotate.
  • Crop pages, add blank ones, and build bookmarks that sit inside each other like chapters.
  • Two-page view, including right to left for Japanese, Arabic and Hebrew books.

Scans and OCR

  • Recognise text in scanned pages, in 56 languages, so you can search, select and copy it.
  • Enhance pages: whiter paper, darker text and sharper letters for faded photocopies.
  • Combine pictures of paper pages into one PDF.
  • Scan straight in from scanners and all-in-one printers: glass or document feeder, one side or both. WIA, TWAIN and network scanners on Windows, SANE on Linux.

Files

  • Compress large PDFs, with a choice of how small.
  • Convert Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, web pages and pictures into PDFs, and PDFs into Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text and pictures.
  • Compare two versions word by word.
  • Add or remove a password, and repair damaged PDFs.
  • Save as PDF/A for long-term archiving.
  • Auto-save, a backup of the original before your first change, and recovery after a crash.

Share securely

  • Lock a copy with a strong code, or so only one person's computer can open it.
  • Send it with a pickup code. They type the code or scan its QR code and the file comes to them, over your Wi-Fi or the internet, encrypted all the way.
  • Anyone without InkLeaf PDF can pick a file up in their browser.

AI, if you want it

  • Summarise a PDF, translate it, or ask it questions with answers that link to the right page.
  • Built-in AI that runs on your own computer: no key, no account, and nothing leaves it.
  • Or use Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity or a server of your own, with your own key.

Part of your computer

  • Thumbnails of each PDF's first page in File Explorer, and every page in its preview pane.
  • Start with Windows, wait in the tray, and open PDFs as soon as you download them.
  • Combine, Print, Compress and Make a PDF in File Explorer's right-click menu.
  • Open again with the files you had open last time.

Your way

  • Tools in a ribbon of labelled tabs, like Word's, or in a slim rail down the side.
  • Light and dark, following your system.
  • The whole app in 30 languages.

Phone

  • An Android app with its own layout, built for reading first, with the editing tools a tap away.
  • Pair it with your computer once and send PDFs both ways over your home Wi-Fi.
