Roadmap

What’s being built, and what comes after. There are no dates: a version ships when it’s ready and the tests pass. Anything under Later is an idea, not a promise.

Now: version 4.0, rebuilt as native apps

InkLeaf PDF is moving from a web-based app to apps made for each kind of device, on one shared engine. Until 4.0 is ready, 3.x keeps getting fixes.

  • A desktop app for Windows and Linux built with Qt (starts faster, uses less memory, and feels like a real desktop program)
  • A new Android app made natively for phones
  • Pages drawn by PDFium, the PDF engine inside Chrome, and InkLeaf’s own engine for editing and saving
  • Every tool from 3.x, on all three
  • Your settings, saved signatures, language packs and AI keys carried over

Next

  • A listing on Flathub
  • Signed Windows installers, with no SmartScreen warning
  • Share securely on the phone
  • Fillable forms on pages merged in from another PDF

Later

  • Mac, then iPhone and iPad
  • Change your edits after saving
  • Edit vertical Chinese, Japanese and Korean text
  • InkLeaf Suite: Docs, and later spreadsheets, slides and notes, as native apps on the same engine, to download one at a time or all together

The full list, with what’s done and where you can help, is in ROADMAP.md. Something missing? Open an issue and say what you’re trying to do.
